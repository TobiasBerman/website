Misc

Below is an unorganized stash of some people, blogs, projects, etc. that I've found interesting or insightful.

- Check out computer graphics legend Inigo Quilez
- In a similar vein, Finnian Macmanus is in my opinion one of the greatest concept artists (and maybe visual artists) of our generation. He also founded Terraform Studios
- Nimrod Kramer is a journalist that seems to really have his finger on the pulse. He uploads videos from within current events while they are happening, and it's usually stuff not picked up by other media.

- woog
- Unrelated, NaissanceE

- Scott Alexander's SSC and ACX go without saying, though the golden age may be over
- Also Scott Aaronson's Shtetl-Optimized for complexity theory / quantum computing stuff

- Highly recommend Vishandel De Poort if ever in Haarlem, Netherlands
